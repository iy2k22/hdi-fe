A list of most countries ranked by their Human Development Index. The index is calculated by measuring three variables:
  • Health
  • Education
  • Standard of Living
It is then measured on a scale between 0 and 1. Most of the data here is sourced from the UNDP, while the data for Macau and Taiwan are sourced from their respective governments. Although the UN's website does provide similar functionality, I didn't like the way they separated countries (by "region"), and I preferred to split by continent. I also wanted to specifically measure Islamic countries, and there was no way to do this on the site.
