Countries ranked by HDI


A list of most countries ranked by their Human Development Index. The index is calculated by measuring three variables:
  • Health
  • Education
  • Standard of Living
It is then measured on a scale between 0 and 1. The data here is sourced from the UNDP. While the UN's website does provide similar functionality, I didn't like the way they separated countries (by "region"), and I preferred to split by continent. I also wanted to specifically measure Islamic countries, and there was no way to do this on the site.
